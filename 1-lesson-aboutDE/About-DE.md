# What do Data Engineers do?
What a business could expect from a data engineer in a data project?

## 1. Be curious about requirements
- Who are the final users of this data?
- What is the level of maturity of the data within the company?
- What do they need to see in order to generate value for the company?
- What about the availability of this data?
- Which are the different data sources and how this data is generated?
- What about data quality? consistency, completeness
